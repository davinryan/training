## Practices
* Always have a goal or thing you want the kids to remember

## Drills
* Attacking drills 1v1, 2v2 (I need to do more)
  * Numbers - Perfect game for 1v1, 2v2
* Defending drills
  * Buzzy bees

## Fundamental Movement
* Spiderman / Crab crawl - use when tagged in drills to get them back in the action
* Use shoulder roll when returning back to a line
* Try more one legged skills to encourage ability to operate when one foot on the ground
  1. Skating hop - zig zag forward on one leg
  2. Single leg stance 
     * pass ball to each other on one leg
     * stand on ball
  3. Ball juggling

* Sole of foot, sideways roll - Roll foot over top of ball to change direction - alternate feet
* Pull ball back - Place obstacle - use same foot on top of ball to roll backwards, capture ball and move forwards away from obstacle
* Behind the leg
  1. Step on ball and roll backwards and behind other leg to change direction
  2. another description: stand on ball, squeeze ball sideways with instep behind other leg
  3. spin 180 degrees on non playing foot, take ball with playing foot
  4. great for keeping ball in play when running up against the line
* Protect the ball
  1. Stand on ball
  2. Roll ball 45 degress back, step back with playing foot, step over ball with other foot
  3. Turn 180 degrees on non playing foot control ball with playing foot
* The flick
  1. stand on ball
  2. roll back, hopping
  3. flick ball into the air with outside of foot either to self or team mate
  4. ball into the air to avoid defenders foot (because defender will try to block but only on the ground)
